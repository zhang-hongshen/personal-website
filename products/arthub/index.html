What it does功能

Add a folder. Arthub does the rest.添加文件夹，剩下的交给 Arthub。

No server to run, no files to import or copy. Arthub reads your folders where they are and builds a library around them. 无需搭建服务器，也不用导入或复制文件。Arthub 直接读取原有文件夹，并据此建立影音库。

Smart recognition智能识别

Understands how you already name files读懂你原本的命名方式

Separators, release tags like 1080p or BluRay, editions and extras are handled for you. Season folders can be Season 01, S01 or even 第一季. 分隔符、1080p、BluRay 等发布标签、版本和花絮都会被自动处理。季文件夹可以叫 Season 01、S01，甚至 第一季。

If a guess is wrong, match it by hand once — the choice is saved per file and survives rescans. 如果识别有误，手动匹配一次即可——结果按文件保存，重新扫描也不会丢失。

Library management资料库管理

Keeps up with your drives跟得上你的硬盘

  • New or changed files are picked up automatically every 30 seconds.每 30 秒自动发现新增或修改的文件。
  • Unplugged a drive? Its metadata stays; playback resumes once it's back.拔掉移动硬盘？元数据依然保留，接回后即可继续播放。
  • Rename or move a file on the same volume and it keeps its match.在同一磁盘内重命名或移动文件，匹配结果不会丢失。
  • Removing a folder only removes the index — never your media.移除文件夹只会删除索引，绝不会动你的媒体文件。
