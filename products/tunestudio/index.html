Auto Tune

Three styles, one click

  • Natural — gently nudges notes toward pitch, so your voice keeps its character.
  • Balanced — puts every held note on pitch and keeps your vibrato.
  • Robotic — snaps notes flat onto pitch for the classic electronic sound.

Simple by default

Open, tune, listen, export

1

Open

Choose a recording of a single voice. Less background music works best.

2

Tune

Click Auto Tune in the toolbar, or drag any note where you want it.

3

Listen

Press Space to play. Not happy? ⌘Z takes you back.

4

Export

Save the result as a new WAV or M4A file.
